Checks it passes

Validated
  • Calibrated to one number: the 1985–2022 trend of −0.0017 pH per year (Copernicus Marine).
  • Untuned: pH change 1850–2022 is −0.125, against a published ≈ −0.1.
  • Untuned: Revelle factor 10.6, inside the typical 8–14 range.
  • Very high scenario reaches pH 7.70 by 2100, in line with published projections.

Be careful with

Caution
  • One global-mean surface box. Cold, upwelling and coastal waters sit well away from 8.04.
  • The shaded range is parameter uncertainty only. It leaves out scenario choice, regional differences and natural variability.
  • Not modelled: changing alkalinity or circulation, biology, minor ions.
  • CO₂ history approximates Mauna Loa; scenarios are SSP-like.

Can a sensor see this?

Sensor

The model repo includes a pH-electrode simulator. A drift of only 0.1 mV per year looks the same as the whole global acidification trend, so detecting it depends on careful calibration.

–

Noise and drift values are simulation inputs, not specs of any real product.
